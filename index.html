Consigli Uses Togal.AI for Takeoffs at Scale

A fifth-generation general contractor with 80–100 estimators across 15+ offices needed real-time collaboration on complex bids. Togal changed how they work — and what they can win.

Consigli Construction Co., Inc. Enterprise 5 min read
Consigli Construction — project site photography

Offices across North America

Enterprise estimating doesn't break because teams are slow. It breaks because they're spread out — multiple estimators working on the same project from different locations, reviewing each other's work without a shared environment, and trying to stay aligned under tight deadlines.

That was Consigli's challenge. A fifth-generation, family-run construction manager operating across more than 15 offices, Consigli regularly collaborates across regions on large, complex projects. Their estimating team — 80 to 100 professionals — needed to work in parallel without stepping on each other's work.

Where legacy takeoff tools fall short at enterprise scale

Before Togal.AI, Consigli used On-Screen Takeoff. For Chief Estimator Tim Ericson, the tool had been a constant for 25 years. But as the team scaled and projects grew more complex, the limitations compounded.

"Before Togal.AI, we used On-Screen Takeoff. I personally have been using it for 25 years. While On-Screen Takeoff did what we needed it to do, we didn't see it making any fundamental changes."

Tim Ericson, Chief Estimator — Consigli Construction

Estimators in headquarters performed well. But remote teams faced lag, delays, and friction completing basic takeoff tasks. On large projects, multiple estimators working simultaneously led to fragmented workflows — harder to maintain alignment, avoid duplication, and ensure complete scope coverage.

Cloud-based takeoff for a more collaborative model

Consigli wasn't looking for a marginally better version of what they had. They were looking for a different way to work — and that's what led them to Togal.AI.

Because Togal.AI is cloud-based, every estimator works in the same environment regardless of location. No remote desktop reliance, no performance gaps between offices, no inconsistency in user experience. Multiple estimators can operate in the same project simultaneously — reviewing each other's work, coordinating in real time, staying aligned as the estimate evolves.

"Our estimating group has around 80 to 100 individuals. And any one of us is utilizing Togal.AI at any given moment. We really see web-based applications as the wave of the future."

Tim Ericson, Chief Estimator — Consigli Construction

Estimators focused on thinking, not clicking

The most meaningful shift wasn't faster takeoffs. It was what faster takeoffs made possible. Reducing repetitive clicking freed estimators to focus on higher-value work: understanding the drawings, identifying risks, building a more complete picture of the project.

The result is a smarter, more comprehensive estimate — and greater confidence in the final numbers.

Building credibility in preconstruction

The impact carries beyond the estimating team. In reconciliation meetings with partners, owners, and subcontractors, Consigli teams can quickly surface their takeoffs, walk through decisions, and clearly explain how quantities were derived.

"We're able to pull it up and show them what we did and why we did it. It gives us a lot more credibility."

Tim Ericson, Chief Estimator — Consigli Construction
